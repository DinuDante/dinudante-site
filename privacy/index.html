Privacy

This is a personal website. It has no account system, no contact form and no advertising or analytics script.

Browser preferences

Your chosen light or dark theme is stored in your browser's local storage under the key dinu-theme. It never leaves your browser. If storage is unavailable or blocked, the site follows your operating-system preference instead and still works normally. Clearing this site's browser data removes the preference.

Visits and measurement

No analytics service, advertising tracker or embedded social player is used. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect its service. See GitHub's general privacy statement.

Product images on the setup page

The setup catalogue displays product photographs served directly by Amazon's image hosts (m.media-amazon.com). Loading that page therefore sends ordinary web requests to Amazon, including your IP address and browser details, in the same way any embedded image does. No other third-party resource is loaded anywhere on this site; fonts, styles and scripts are all served from this domain.

Affiliate links

Product links on the setup page are Amazon affiliate links. If you buy something after following one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate participation does not change what appears in the catalogue: it lists hardware I bought and use. Two recently added items are marked as newly added and not yet long-term tested. Amazon's own privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Contact and external sites

Email, WhatsApp, social links and links to ProLEAP Academy or DDPrinterZ open their respective services, each with its own privacy practices. Any message you choose to send is handled by that service; this website does not collect or store its contents.
